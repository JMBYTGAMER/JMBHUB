01

Overview

JMB HUB is an independent portal for games, utilities, Minecraft tools, support, account features and community experiences. These policies explain the main rules and information that may be processed when you use those features.

Some features are optional. The information processed depends on the pages and functions you choose to use. This page is a plain-language site policy, not legal advice.

02

Copyright & trademarks

Unless a page says otherwise, the original JMB HUB name, logo, page design, original graphics, text and source code are project materials. Do not copy, republish, sell or redistribute substantial parts of those materials without permission.

Names, logos, game titles, brands and other materials belonging to third parties remain the property of their respective owners. They are used to identify related services or features. Their appearance does not mean those owners endorse JMB HUB.

If you submit text, images or campaign material to a JMB HUB feature, you confirm that you have permission to provide it. You keep your rights; you grant JMB HUB only the limited permission needed to store, review and display that material for the feature you used.

03

Privacy & information

JMB HUB uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for account access and selected portal features. Depending on what you use, information may include:

  • Account identifiers and sign-in details handled by Firebase Authentication, such as your email address and Firebase user ID.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, including your display name, profile preferences and optional birthday day/month.
  • Feature records such as game scores, virtual coin balances and transactions, support requests, submitted ad campaigns and account settings.
  • For private calls, temporary room and signalling information needed to connect participants.
  • Basic interaction logs such as page visited, event type, timestamp and selected game/tool label. If the site owner enables the highest telemetry mode, relative pointer positions may also be recorded for interface diagnostics.

JMB HUB uses this information to operate the portal, protect accounts, provide requested features, investigate abuse, troubleshoot faults and maintain service security. JMB HUB does not intentionally sell personal account information.

Firebase and other providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. JMB HUB does not control the data handling of external services once you use them.

Current Firebase Storage rules disable direct file uploads through JMB HUB. This may change only if an upload feature is introduced with its own clear rules.

04

Browser storage

The site and its service providers may use browser storage, such as local storage, session storage or Firebase Authentication persistence, to keep you signed in, remember interface preferences and support session features. You can clear site data in your browser, but doing so may sign you out or reset local preferences.

Do not use a shared or public device for a private account unless you can sign out and clear its saved session afterwards.

05

Accounts & profiles

Keep your password private and use only your own account. Do not impersonate another person, share an administrator account or attempt to bypass account restrictions. You are responsible for activity carried out through a session you control.

Some profile fields may be shown to other users when you choose a public-facing feature. Birthday displays are designed to show the display name and day/month only, not the birth year. Avoid placing sensitive information in public profile fields or support messages.

Administrators may review account and service data when needed to provide support, moderate features, investigate security incidents or enforce these policies.

06

Private calls

Private Calls request access to your camera and microphone only when you choose to start or join a call and approve the browser permission prompt. The feature uses browser real-time communication and Firebase-based signalling to help participants connect.

JMB HUB does not provide an in-app call recording feature. Room and signalling records may be processed to establish, maintain and clean up a call. Participants should not record or share another person's call without their permission and should end a call when finished.

07

JMB Coins & rewards

JMB Coins are in-site virtual points used by supported JMB HUB features. They are not cash, cryptocurrency, an investment or a bank balance, and have no guaranteed monetary value or cash redemption. Reward rules, availability and eligibility may change; an award is valid only when the relevant JMB HUB feature confirms it.

Do not sell, trade or claim that JMB Coins can be exchanged for real-world money unless a separate official JMB HUB announcement explicitly says so.

08

Acceptable use & security tools

Use JMB HUB lawfully and respectfully. Do not attempt unauthorised access, disrupt the service, exploit another user's account, interfere with moderation or misuse community features.

Security and developer tools are for learning and for systems you own or have clear permission to assess. A tool result is informational and is not a guarantee that a website, password or system is secure. Do not use the tools to access or damage systems without permission.

Do not upload, submit or share material that you do not have the right to use, that violates another person's privacy, or that is unlawful or abusive.

09

Third-party services & links

JMB HUB may link to or rely on services such as Google Firebase, GitHub, Discord, game platforms and other external websites. Those services have their own rules, availability and privacy policies. Opening an external link may share ordinary connection information with that provider.

Third-party names, logos, content and trademarks remain with their respective owners. JMB HUB does not claim ownership of external content and cannot guarantee that an external page remains available or safe.

10

Availability & disclaimer

JMB HUB is an evolving project. Features may be changed, paused or removed for maintenance, safety or technical reasons. We work to keep the portal useful, but do not promise uninterrupted operation, error-free results or permanent availability of third-party services.

Tools, calculators and security checks provide general information only. Check important results independently and seek qualified advice for high-impact decisions.

11

Copyright, privacy & support requests

For a copyright concern, privacy question, account-data request or report about a page, provide the relevant page address and a clear description. Do not include your password, one-time codes or other secret credentials.

You can send a request through the JMB HUB Support page after signing in, or open an issue in the JMB HUB GitHub repository. Requests are reviewed manually. Account and data requests may require proof that you control the account.

These policies may be updated as JMB HUB features change. The effective date at the top of this page shows the latest published revision.
