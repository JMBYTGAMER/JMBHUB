Keep calls private and comfortable

Only call people you already know and trust. Do not post invitation links publicly or share your address, school, passwords or other private details. You can end a call at any time.

JMB HUB has no call-recording control. Other participants may still record with their own devices. Camera and microphone access are requested only when you start or join. Call setup metadata (SDP/ICE connection details) is exchanged through Firebase; the media stream is sent directly between browsers when the network allows it. A STUN service may see network-address metadata.

Connection note: this first version uses direct browser-to-browser media. Some mobile or restricted networks may block direct connections; reliable relay support (TURN) has not yet been configured.
