JMB COINS EXPLAINED

Virtual credits, not real money

JMB Coins are in-site credits for supported portal features. They are not cash, cryptocurrency, a bank balance or a guaranteed reward.

  1. Open JMB Coins to check your wallet and transaction history.
  2. Only use a reward or claim action when the portal shows it as available.
  3. For an admin gift, open the notification inbox and review the message before collecting.
  4. If an action reports an error, save the exact text and contact Support. Avoid repeated attempts.

Some reward, gift and account-management actions need the secure Firebase Cloud Functions backend to be deployed. If those services are not deployed or are offline, the related action may be unavailable.

PRIVACY & SECURITY

Keep your account safe

  • Never share your password, email verification code, one-time code, wallet recovery phrase or private key.
  • Do not paste confidential information into online tools or AI services.
  • On a shared computer or phone, sign out after using JMB HUB.
  • Use only the official website address and trusted links.
  • Birthday sharing is optional. Review what will be public before enabling it.
  • Report suspicious activity or unexpected account changes through Support.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers

Why can't I open a page?

Some pages require sign-in. Open Login, confirm you are using the correct account, then return to the page. A feature may also be unavailable during maintenance.

Why did a JMB Coin reward fail?

Check the exact error and your transaction history. Reward and gift actions depend on the secure backend being available. Contact Support instead of repeating a failed claim.

Why does the page look different on my phone?

JMB HUB adapts to screen size. Use the menu on mobile, or the navigation sidebar and wider workspace on desktop.

Can I share my birthday?

Yes, but it is optional. The public celebration list is designed to show your display name and day/month only. Avoid sharing anything you do not want public.

How do I report a bug?

Open Support and describe what you were doing, the page name, and the exact error text. Never include passwords or sign-in codes.

Who can use the Admin panel?

Admin access is restricted to authorised accounts. Some owner-only actions also require server-side Cloud Functions. Never try to bypass access checks.

STILL NEED HELP?

Tell the support team what happened.

Include the page, the steps you took, and the exact error message. Keep private account information out of your ticket.
