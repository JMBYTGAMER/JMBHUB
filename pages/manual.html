Help & Guide

Simple instructions for using the portal on phone or computer.

🚀 Getting started

  1. Open JMB HUB and choose Get Started.
  2. Sign in or create your account.
  3. Open Dashboard to reach Games, Tools, Minecraft, AI, Support and Coins.
  4. On a phone, use the menu button to move between sections.

🎮 Playing games & earning

  1. Open JMB Games and choose a game.
  2. Read its description and complete a score-earning round.
  3. Supported completed rounds can award 1 virtual JMB Coin, subject to a 15-second reward cooldown.
  4. Check your wallet and history in JMB Coins.

JMB Coins are virtual site credits, not cash. Game scores are for the portal leaderboard.

🧰 Using tools

  1. Open JMB Area and search for a tool.
  2. Tap a tool card to open its workspace.
  3. Read the description under the tool name, enter your content and use the action buttons.
  4. Some tools run locally in your browser. Keep a copy of important input before leaving.

🪙 Collecting admin rewards

  1. Look for the inbox button on the portal.
  2. Open the reward message.
  3. Tap Collect reward once and wait for confirmation.
  4. Check the wallet balance after the collection.

If an error appears, do not keep retrying. Open Support and include the exact error code.

🔊 Sound

Use the small floating sound control near the bottom-right. Tap it to switch sound on or off. The preference is saved on that device. A browser may require a tap before it allows audio.

🎂 Birthday announcement

The owner can enable or disable the homepage announcement, select its India-local date, and add a title and special message from Admin → Birthday.

🛠️ Full maintenance

When enabled, regular visitors see a full-screen maintenance page. Verified admins and the owner can still browse the portal and open Admin. The owner can switch the site back online from Admin → Services.

🆘 Need help?

Open Support, create a ticket, explain what happened and include the exact error. Never share your password or sign-in codes.
